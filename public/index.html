The Problem

Feedback is scattered.
Real problems stay buried.

Scattered channels

Google reviews, app stores, tickets, WhatsApp, emails, QR forms, social media and call transcripts — in many languages.

Hidden failures

A broken freezer, a mislabelled allergen, rude billing staff or payment failures stay buried until they become major operational failures.

Real risk

Head-office teams, store managers and customers are affected — with food-safety, revenue and reputation risk.

The Solution

An operations command center,
not a chatbot.

StoreSignal ingests multi-channel feedback, detects critical issues, ranks them by severity and routes them to the right owner — with evidence quotes and recommended actions.
